## 👩‍🌾 About Me

I am an **Agricultural Engineer** from the **National University of Colombia** <img src="https://flagcdn.com/w20/co.png" width="20" alt="Colombia"/>
and hold an **M.Sc. in Agricultural Engineering** from the **University of Campinas (UNICAMP)** <img src="https://flagcdn.com/w20/br.png" width="20" alt="Brazil"/>,
with a focus on **Digital Agriculture**.

I am currently a **PhD candidate at UNICAMP** <img src="https://flagcdn.com/w20/br.png" width="20" alt="Brazil"/>,
working in **Digital and Precision Agriculture**.

My research focuses on the integration of **spatial data, geostatistics, remote sensing, machine learning, and on-farm experimentation** to understand agricultural variability and support site-specific management decisions.

My master's research focused on developing a framework to guide the selection of **spatial interpolation methods** for digital soil mapping under different data characteristics and soil sampling conditions.
